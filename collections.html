How Mm/Mw Works

The MM/MW process helps make transactions more organized, clear, and secure for both the buyer and seller. As the middleman/midwoman, I help guide the transaction by checking the agreed details, receiving and verifying the item or payment, and releasing it to the other party once everything is confirmed. This step-by-step process helps prevent misunderstandings and gives both parties a trusted person to assist them throughout the transaction.
